Megalodon Macropad Map

The pad lights up in the layer's colour, so you can see where you are: blue Base, orange Cursor, green Terminal, purple Obsidian. Keys that do nothing on a layer stay dark. Press the right small dial to step to the next layer, and the big dial to jump home to Base. The top row is the same on every layer.

Bind these in Obsidian

Settings → Hotkeys, search the command, click the plus, then press the pad key.

  • Daily notes: Open today's daily note Meh+D
  • Templates: Insert template Meh+I
  • Toggle left sidebar Meh+[
  • Toggle right sidebar Meh+]
  • Split right Meh+\

Why the terminal layer is different

  • Copy, paste and find use Ctrl+Shift, since Ctrl+C stops the running command.
  • Save is off. Ctrl+S freezes Ptyxis output; Ctrl+Q unfreezes it.
  • The big dial scrolls, because Ctrl+Z suspends the running program.
  • Clear is typed as clear so Cursor's Ctrl+L chat can't grab it.
